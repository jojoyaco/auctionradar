# Licensing and noncommercial-use policy

The project owner has chosen to prohibit **all profit-oriented use**, not only
charging for a copy of the software. This includes using AuctionRadar to find or
evaluate auctions, purchases, investments, or resales with the intention of
earning a profit or commercial return. The [LICENSE](../LICENSE) expresses this
policy through a custom project license.

## What the license permits and restricts

- Use, study, modification, and redistribution are permitted without a license
  fee, solely for noncommercial purposes.
- There is no blanket exception for charities, educational bodies, governments,
  research organizations, or internal business use.
- The license prohibits direct or indirect financial exploitation, including
  paid copies, SaaS, hosting, APIs, support, integrations, advertising,
  commissions, or monetizing the software's outputs.
- Using the software to identify or evaluate auctions, purchases, investments,
  or resales with an intention of earning a return is also prohibited, even if
  the software itself is free.
- Distributed modified versions must retain the same restrictions, include the
  corresponding preferred source code, and preserve applicable notices.
- The license does not provide automatic conversion to a permissive or
  commercial license, or a paid commercial exception.

The license covers only original project material and rights that contributors
can license. It does not change the independent terms of third-party libraries,
auction listings, images, documents, or data.

## Why this is a custom license

MIT, Apache, and GPL licenses permit commercial use. PolyForm Noncommercial
1.0.0 includes specific permissions for certain organizations regardless of
funding and does not express this project's intended restriction in identical
terms. The project has not modified another license while retaining its name:
its text uses a project-specific `LicenseRef` and is **not presented as a
standard license**.

The restriction is incompatible with the Open Source Definition. The code is
source-available, but the license is not OSI-approved and does not grant
commercial rights. It may also prevent acceptance into catalogs that require an
open-source license, including a possible Proxmox Helper-Scripts review.

## Legal review

The license is an initial formulation of the selected policy and needs review by
a qualified legal professional in the relevant jurisdictions before broad
publication. This guide is not legal advice. No license can guarantee that its
terms will be enforceable in every circumstance or override rights granted by
mandatory applicable law.

The noncommercial terms apply to versions offered under this license. They do
not automatically cover future works that a rights holder has not offered under
these terms, relicense third-party dependencies, or grant rights over third-
party material.
